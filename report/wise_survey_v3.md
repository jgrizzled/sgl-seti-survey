## Summary

We searched 14.5 years of WISE/NEOWISE single-exposure imaging
(2010–2024) for compact persistent sources on the solar
gravitational-lens focal lines of nearby stellar systems. Report v2
closed the 8 pc, nearest-neighbour portfolio (47 endpoints). This
version adds the **picky-network expansion**: the hypothesis
(`notes/picky_network_hypothesis_sgl_seti.md`) that a selective
interstellar network skips unattractive nearby systems — close
binaries, active or evolved hosts — and links instead to
engineering-favourable, graph-valuable, or scientifically interesting
systems farther out. The universal target list v2 encodes that as a
10 pc census with new **engineering-backbone** and **science-interest**
baskets and a desirability-filtered graph re-vote, and the WISE
overlay queued 35 new systems. Together with four formerly
orbit-blocked components, batch 5 added 41 endpoints across 39
corridors. **The full v2 portfolio is now searched: 88 endpoint
hypotheses across 77 corridors (176 endpoint-role hypotheses), and no
candidate survives.** Forty new threshold excesses were adjudicated
by a now-tracked census tool and vetoed — overwhelmingly single-phase
static field stars, the remainder single-visit cryo cells,
single-epoch artifacts, and dense-corridor coincidences. Seventy exceeding
cells under the current run — every batch, re-censused uniformly — are
individually vetoed. Every
endpoint carries 90%-recovery flux limits over 550–10,000 AU in all
four WISE bands — now on a corrected flux scale: the shared matched
filter had reported PSF peak amplitude rather than total flux, so all
depths quoted in Reports v1–v2 were optimistic by 1.9 mag (W1–W3) and
3.3 mag (W4). Detection statistics, thresholds and every veto are
invariant to that scale; the magnitudes and physical size limits below
are the corrected ones.

A selection-level result stands on its own: once binaries, active and
evolved hosts are pruned from the local graph, the Sun's natural
neighbours are all still within 4 pc — so under a picky network the
far-shell coverage rests entirely on the engineering and science
baskets, and the engineering ranking independently reproduced the
systems the hypothesis note had singled out (σ Dra, 82 Eri,
HD 219134, GJ 876, Luyten's Star, Ross 128).

## 1. Search definition

Unchanged from v1 (frozen 2026-08-18): station-kept relay on the
Sun–star focal line; Rx and Tx as separate hypotheses; relay distance
550–10,000 AU (log-uniform prior); unresolved point source; duty
cycle ≥ 0.5; residual motion |µ| ≤ 1″/yr; 99% propagated confidence
+ 10″ padding; Earth-center observer (declared ~2.5 mas
approximation). Out of scope (separate model IDs): barycenter and
planetary endpoints, duty < 0.5, |µ| > 1″/yr, extended morphologies.

**Endpoints (88).** v1.0–v1.4 as in Report v2 (47: the nearest-star
portfolio incl. α Cen, Sirius, Procyon, Luhman 16 orbits and the
ε Ind Ba/Bb and EZ Aqr photocentre tracks). **v1.5 (41):** GJ 876,
GJ 1002, GJ 832, GJ 526, GJ 581, GJ 514, Fomalhaut, Wolf 437, GJ 915,
GJ 518, GJ 1276, 61 Vir, GJ 2012, GJ 11547, LHS 1723, 82 Eri,
GJ 338 A/B, GJ 625, GJ 293, GJ 3306, GJ 3112, GJ 687, GJ 674, GJ 682,
GJ 251, σ Dra, HD 219134, GJ 1087, GJ 318, Wolf 1069, GJ 588, GJ 3512,
GJ 13157, GJ 2066, GJ 367, and the unblocked components GJ 229 A,
GJ 667 C, LTT 1445 A, GJ 66 A/B. Curation: entries generated by a
tracked script from Gaia DR3 (Hipparcos-2 for the Gaia-saturated
Fomalhaut), each carrying the universal-list basket labels that
motivated it; 82 Eri and σ Dra with ×3-inflated uncertainties
(saturation RUWE, clean proper-motion anomalies); nine white dwarfs
with the 0 ± 50 km/s RV allocation.

## 2. Target selection: universal list v2 + survey overlay

`targets/universal_v2` (76 systems / 95 tracks within 10 pc) keeps
v1's protected distance core, geometric-neighbour, historical and
white-dwarf baskets and adds the picky-network machinery:

- **Engineering layer** (`census/engineering_*.csv`): mass/radius
  (Mann M_Ks relations, TIC, FLAME, with provenance), lens
  compactness M/R², companion acceleration (Hipparcos–Gaia
  proper-motion anomaly, HGCA, RUWE), activity (log R'HK, CARMENES Hα,
  ROSAT Lx/Lbol with upper limits), evolutionary state — each with
  measured / limit / unknown recorded; *unknown is never favourable*.
- **Desirability levels** (the network's presumed taste) replace v1's
  selective re-vote filter, which had conflated our solvability with
  the network's preferences. Result: the desirable-subgraph re-vote
  adds no system beyond the core.
- **Engineering Backbone:** rank-sum of compactness, stellar power,
  quietness, dynamical cleanliness, lifetime and distance among
  measured-quiet, level-2 dwarfs; 12 non-core additions.
- **Science Interest:** a curated, referenced YAML (23 tier-1,
  14 tier-2 systems — temperate terrestrials, multi-planet and
  resonant systems, debris architectures).
- Fixes surfaced by the expansion: decimal GJ ids (v1's "GJ 54" is
  YZ Cet, GJ 54.1 — the registry endpoint `gj-54` is YZ Cet and its
  search stands), projected-separation system grouping (GJ 667), RUWE
  ignored for Gaia-saturated stars, proper-motion anomaly taking
  precedence over HGCA χ² for the highest-proper-motion stars.

The WISE overlay (`overlay_v2`) measured confusion for every new
corridor and ordered the queue (13 low, 8 mid, 9 high, 5 bright-star).
As in earlier batches the grades were predictive: low-confusion
corridors (GJ 66, GJ 625, GJ 514) reached W1 m₉₀ ≈ 15.6–15.9 — the
survey's deepest, on a par with Lacaille 8760 and YZ Cet (15.6) once
all batches sit on the corrected scale; the high-confusion HD 219134
and GJ 13157 corridors bottomed at 12.0–12.6.

## 4. Search results and adjudications

**Catalog screening** produced ~90–6,500 chance matches per visit per
endpoint-role (the upper end in the GJ 687 ecliptic-pole corridor);
every fixed-z recurrence peak resolved as a static background star via
the **parallax-phase test** (batch 5: 2,594 of 2,597 recurring
fixed-position clusters confined to one day-of-year window; the three
exceptions sit in GJ 687's ecliptic-pole corridor, where the track
revisits the same field, and a fixed-sky-position cluster is not
track-following by construction).

**Forced-photometry stacks** — matched-filter fluxes stacked jointly
over a 64-node relay-distance grid (uniform in 1/z) × 5×5
residual-motion grid, thresholded per endpoint-role-band at the
maximum of **8 offset-control trajectories** (empirical FAR < 1/8 per
grid search; contamination-limited, with control maxima up to
S ≈ 2,650).

**Uniform census under `run-1b2e86e9219e`: 70 of 704 pair-band
searches across all five batches exceed threshold; all vetoed.** The
exceedance census is now a tracked tool
(`surveys/wise/scripts/adjudicate_exceedances.py`) that recomputes the
phase split, single-epoch share, companion-band significance and the
nearest and brightest CatWISE sources at each phase position for every
exceeding cell, and writes Candidate records; a cell that fails no veto
is written `retained` for manual review. One was — luyten-star/tx/W1
(S = 319 vs T = 238, both phases elevated, W1:W2 = 1.46, no source
within 6″) — and manual review found a W1 = 9.1 star 3–4″ from the
phase-1 track position and a W1 = 8.6 star 11″ from the phase-0
position, with the best fit at the residual-motion grid edge and a
rise-and-decay light curve (absent in 2010, peak 2015–2017, gone by
2023) as the drifting trajectory crosses their PSF wings; a station-kept
relay at duty ≥ 0.5 is persistent by hypothesis. That bright-star-halo
test is now a rule in the tool.

| Mechanism | Batch-5 example |
| --- | --- |
| Single-phase static field star (the parallax-phase veto) — 35 cells | gj-625 rx+tx, W1+W2: the batch's strongest excess (S = 127 vs T = 67) with phase split 0.4 / 126.9, W1:W2 = 3.8, and a CatWISE W1 = 14.1 star 3.5″ from the phase-1 track position |
| Single-visit W3/W4 cryo cell — 15 cells | gj-13157 rx/W3: S₀ = −4.5 / S₁ = 257; nine W4 cells at S ≈ T |
| Single-epoch dominance (> 50% of the capped stack) — 2 cells | gj-318 rx/W1 (54%), gj-526 rx/W3 (65%) |
| Balanced phases from *distinct* static sources (dense-corridor class) — 3 cells | sigma-dra rx/W1 (+1.5%): W1 = 18.2 star 1″ from the phase-0 position and a W1 = 12.3 star 5″ from the phase-1 position |
| Bright-star halo with a transient light curve (new rule) — 1 cell | luyten-star tx/W1: see above |

The v2 mechanisms (bright-star halo, the single-epoch artifact closed
by the v0.2.0 weight cap, cross-band mimicry by a static star's own
colours) recur unchanged.

## 5. Completeness

Injection recovery (analytic Gaussian-source injections at every grid
node, randomized duty realizations, recovery = exceeding the
predeclared threshold at the true cell) yields 90%-recovery depths
per endpoint × role × band × z-interval:

![90% recovery depth by endpoint and band](figures/depth_by_endpoint.svg)

**Erratum and rescale (2026-08-20/21).** The ZTF asteroid positive
control revealed that `sglsurvey.photometry` built its matched-filter
kernel with unit *peak*, so its "flux" was PSF peak amplitude; every
magnitude derived from it in Reports v1–v2 was too faint by
Δm = 2.5 log₁₀(2πσ²) = 1.87 / 1.97 / 2.01 / 3.34 mag in W1 / W2 / W3 /
W4 (`surveys/wise/results/erratum_flux_scale_2026-08-20.md`). S/N,
control thresholds, recovery fractions and verdicts are scale-invariant.
All 176 sample tensors were rebuilt under the unit-sum kernel and
recalibrated (v0.2.1, `run-1b2e86e9219e`); the figure and the numbers
in this report are the corrected ones. Survey-wide corrected medians
(Rx): W1 13.9, W2 13.0, W3 10.6, W4 7.1; best W1 15.9 (GJ 66 B),
worst 11.2 (Luhman 16 B); best W3 12.8 (GJ 625).

Depth is set by corridor crowding and bright-star artifacts, not
photon noise. Full 64-node curves:
`runs/wise/calib_v1/m90_curves.npz`; per-interval values in the 5,632
Constraint records; figure generated by
`surveys/wise/scripts/plot_depths.py`.

The survey constrains **warm, actively powered structures** at the
~100 km scale at the near focal line (via the 2010 cryo W3 epochs) and
hot or nonthermal emission over the 14.5-yr baseline; it does **not**
constrain cold passive infrastructure or planet-scale reflectors.

## 7. Caveats

As in v2 (Gaussian-PSF search/injections; 8 controls per pair-band;
single-visit W3/W4; phase-veto power loss in the densest corridors;
no moving-object positive control; nulls cover the frozen cell only),
plus: Reports v1–v2 and Constraint records under AnalysisRuns through
`run-935f8d521a31` carry peak-amplitude magnitudes (subtract Δm above
to compare; superseded by `run-1b2e86e9219e`); batch 5's
coarse/precise records carry the `v1.4` hypothesis
label (the pin was bumped after launch; physics identical, and per
the v1.1 addendum earlier-pinned records remain valid — the
AnalysisRun and constraints pin v1.5); 0.5% of batch-5 cutouts are
missing; the engineering basket depends on archival activity
indicators whose coverage is uneven (142 log R'HK, 69 Hα, 181 ROSAT
detections over 386 census rows) — *unknown* stars were excluded from
that basket rather than assumed quiet.

## Erratum v3.1 (2026-08-22) — claims withdrawn pending the v2 re-analysis

This erratum responds to the scientific review of 2026-08-21
(`surveys/wise/scientific_review.md`) and is the first step of the v2
programme (`surveys/wise/v2_plan.md` §0, §9.1; `notes/project_plan.md`
§10). **No number in this report has been recomputed.** The search,
its data products, and its records stand; what changes is what they
are claimed to establish. The status line of this report should be
read as: *exploratory search, no compelling candidate after the
survey's heuristic review rules; calibrated exclusion pending v2
(`report/wise_survey_v4.md`).*

Withdrawn:

1. **"90 %-complete exclusion" across the stated cell.** The §5
   recovery curves are *threshold-crossing* sensitivity for analytic
   Gaussian injections added to sampled tensors at grid nodes, under
   the independent-flicker duty model only. They do not include the
   candidate vetoes of §4, do not cover the space between grid nodes
   except through an analytic mismatch factor, and carry no
   confidence interval (32 repeats per node). The 5,632 Constraint
   records under `run-1b2e86e9219e` are to be read with
   `completeness_kind = threshold` (a field added to the schema for
   v2; v1 records carry it by supersession link, not by rewrite), and
   their `z_interval_au` blocks leave 9.2 % of the log-uniform prior
   mass uncovered between blocks.
2. **"Empirical FAR < 1/8 per grid search".** The 8-control maximum
   is a per-search rank statistic: a noise-only cell exceeds it with
   probability ≈ 1/9, so ≈ 78 of the 704 searches were expected to
   cross and 70 did. The threshold therefore controlled no survey-wide
   false-alarm rate, and the §4 sentence "empirical FAR < 1/8 per grid
   search" and the `far_note` on every Constraint are withdrawn. The
   replacement statement, per cell, is the *rank statement*: the real
   trajectory's rank among its 8 exchangeable controls, which for a
   "70 of 704 exceed" census is consistent with the null at the
   per-search level and says nothing about the survey-wide level
   (calibrated in v2 with a ~300-trial null ensemble per cell and a
   family-wise error rate).
3. **End-to-end W3/W4 exclusions (§6: "~99 km, 300 K at 550 AU").**
   326 of 352 W3/W4 cells are single-visit cryo cells (< 60
   exposures), which the adjudicator vetoed automatically; a real
   source in such a cell would have been vetoed too. The §6 table is
   relabelled **raw threshold sensitivity for a monochromatic Gaussian
   source**; no exclusion of warm structures follows from it.
4. **"99 % confidence locus" and "every stage content-addressed".**
   The production precise pass evaluated the *nominal* locus and
   recorded `confidence_level = 0.99` as metadata; the seeded
   Monte-Carlo propagation was never called in production. AnalysisRun
   identities hashed tensor *filenames*, not contents, and
   `observation_set_hash` / `intersection_set_hash` on the calibration
   runs are the literal string `see-tensor-manifest`. §9's "every
   stage emits immutable content-addressed records" is true of the
   Observation / IntersectionEvaluation / ScreenMatch streams and
   overstated for the calibration products.

Corrected:

5. **Observer arithmetic (§1).** WISE's ~525 km *altitude* is not its
   geocentric radius; the Earth-centre approximation is bounded by
   ≈ 6,900 km / 550 AU ≈ **17 mas**, not 2.5 mas. Still negligible
   against the 6″ PSF; v2 uses the spacecraft state from the L1b
   headers anyway.
6. **Vetoes (§4).** Phase split, single-epoch dominance, W1:W2
   significance ratio, single-visit cryo, "+< 10 % marginal" and
   bare CatWISE proximity are **heuristic review rules** whose
   selection functions were never measured; the 70 "vetoed" cells are
   *closed by heuristic review*, not rejected by calibrated tests. In
   particular the W1:W2 significance ratio is not a colour and the
   single-visit cryo rule rejects the expected signature of a real
   source.
7. **Motion bound.** The search grid is component-wise
   (|µ*α|, |µ*δ| ≤ 1″/yr, an L∞ box); §1's "|µ| ≤ 1″/yr" should be
   read that way. v2 declares it explicitly.
8. **Scope.** The survey is targeted coverage of a frozen 88-endpoint
   portfolio; nothing here constrains the prevalence of SGL relays or
   the Picky-Network hypothesis.
9. **W4 kernel width (found 2026-08-22 while building v2).**
   `sglsurvey.photometry` assumed 2.75″ pixels for every band, but W4
   L1b frames are 508 × 508 at 5.52″/pixel, so the v1 W4 matched
   filter was twice too wide in pixels (8.7 px ≈ 24″ FWHM instead of
   12″). Thresholds, controls and the analytic injections used the same
   kernel, so S/N-based verdicts are self-consistent; the W4 depths are
   less sensitive than the PSF allows. v2 takes the pixel scale from
   the frame header; v1's default is kept unchanged for reproducibility.

What remains established: the frozen trajectories of 88 endpoints ×
2 roles were searched in all four bands with the implemented
nominal-locus, L∞-motion, Gaussian matched-filter stack; 70 cells
exceeded the 8-control maximum, consistent with the rank expectation
(≈ 78); none remained compelling after heuristic review. The v2
re-analysis (`surveys/wise-v2/`) re-uses every frame, cutout and
record of this survey and replaces the statistical experiment.

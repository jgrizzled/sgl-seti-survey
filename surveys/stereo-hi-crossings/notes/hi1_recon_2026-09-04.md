# STEREO-A HI-1 recon (plan §5.15 item O2; follows LASCO §5.11)

Goal: DECam-style reachability recon for a STEREO-A Heliospheric Imager
HI-1 crossings survey of the sunward channels S1 (downlink post-lens)
and S2 (uplink past the Sun), the second heliospheric substrate after
LASCO. Prerequisite (plan): a STEREO-A observer crossing list.

## 1. Observer crossing list `crossings/stereoa_v1` (built first)

`sglsurvey/crossings.py` gained `--observer stereoa` /
`--fetch-observer stereoa` (Horizons **−234**, the Kepler/SOHO pattern
verbatim). Table `crossings/observers/stereoa_sc_ephemeris.npz`
(29,097 rows, 6 h, 2007-01-01 → 2026-12-01; raw response + sha
pinned). Era stop is bounded by the Horizons predicted-trajectory end
**2026-12-18** (`ahead_2026_232_01.epm`; fit to tracking through
2026-08-20) — extend at the yearly refresh. Product
`crossings/stereoa_v1` (`xng-3dd5b764776e`): **7,516 events, 0
invalid, 195 degraded** (window-boundary minima), manifest parity.

Census + validation (`scripts/hi_census.py` →
`results/stereoa_census_v1.json`):

- **Earth-center is invalid at every rung** (as for Kepler): matched
  same-target/direction/side events shift by |Δt_ca| median 51 d
  (up to the 120-d match limit; 1,241 events unmatched within 120 d —
  the spacecraft was 100°–180° from Earth in 2011–2019). Impact
  parameters of the sunward family differ too (gj-1276 S1: 0.37 R☉
  from STEREO-A vs 0.76 R☉ from Earth).
- Sunward rungs (2007–2026): S1 ≤ 1.2 R☉ 84 events / 4 targets;
  ≤ 2.5 R☉ 105 / 5; **S1 0.1 AU 147 / 7 and S2 0.1 AU 148 / 7** (21 per
  target per channel — STEREO-A's synodic year is 346 d). Same seven
  systems as LASCO: van-maanen (S1 min b **0.09 R☉**), gj-1276 (0.36),
  teegarden (1.06), wolf-359 (1.06), ross-128 (1.50), ross-154 (3.6),
  gj-908 (11.4). S2 1 AU: 1,905 / 88.
- **Apparent sources are fixed ICRS directions** for both channels
  (`scripts/hi_geometry.py`): S2 = the star, S1 = the antipode (the
  LASCO graze-point construction reduces to it exactly at the 0.1 AU
  rung: src − obs = −s·â). No z-grid.

## 3. Product facts (real frames, `runs/stereo-hi-crossings/recon/`)

- **Level 1 `14h1A`** (20100615_000901, 004901): 1024 × 1024 float32,
  `BUNIT = DN/s/CCDPIX`, EXPTIME 1199.94 s (`N_IMAGES = 30` summed
  40-s exposures, `SUMMED = 2` binning), cadence 40 min (36/day),
  DATE-AVG mid-time. Processing per HISTORY: desmeared, flat-fielded
  (`20100421_flatfld_sum_h1a`), "divided by 4 for IPSUM"; RAL star-fit
  pointing (`hi_point_v2`, ATT_FILE). **Saturated columns are NaN**
  (5,120 px = 5 columns in the test frame). Header carries **two
  WCSs**: primary `HPLN/HPLT-AZP` (helioprojective, CRVAL = (−14.02°,
  −0.16°), PC = identity, CDELT 0.019980° = 72″/px, PV2_1 0.102) and
  the alternate **`RA---AZP/DEC--AZP` ('A')** celestial system with a
  rotation matrix. Spacecraft state vectors (HCI/HEE, DSUN_OBS) in the
  header. Some headers carry duplicated COMMENT/HISTORY blocks
  (astropy VerifyWarning on `BLANK`, tolerated).
- **Level 2 `24h1A_br01`**: same header/WCS; median 0.02 DN/s
  (background removed) vs L1 7.7 DN/s.
- Footprint (helioprojective, from the primary WCS): **HPLN −24.15° →
  −3.90°, HPLT −10.29° → +9.97°** — the camera looks 20° east of the
  Sun, the side on which fixed directions approach conjunction.

## 4. Astrometry + photometry validation (the go/no-go check)

`scripts/recon_star_check.py` → `results/recon_star_check_v1.json`
(L1 frame 2010-06-15 00:09 UT, 31-px median high-pass, top-hat
aperture r 2.5 px / annulus 5–9 px, Hipparcos V ≤ 9.5):

- **Header celestial WCS is sub-pixel:** 694 of 698 Hipparcos stars in
  the field detected within 3 px of prediction, residual **0.34 px rms
  per axis (25″), mean offset (0.01, 0.04) px** — no synthetic WCS or
  roll resolution needed (the LASCO chain's hardest part is free here).
- **Photometry:** ZP (V = ZP − 2.5 log₁₀ DN/s) = 11.10 at B−V = 0.65
  with a **colour coefficient +0.595 mag/(B−V)** (668 calibrators at
  S/N ≥ 10); per-star scatter **0.27 → 0.08 mag** after the colour
  term (HI-1 is a 630–730 nm band). No magnitude dependence V 5–10.
  The freeze-chain implementation (`scripts/hi_lib.py`: 2-D quadratic
  ZP, colour-corrected) gives 0.047 mag MAD on 611 calibrators, flat
  to 0.04 mag across the field.
- **L2/L1 parity:** aperture flux ratio 1.005 ± 0.010 (668 stars);
  chain ZPs 11.069 (L1) vs 11.085 (L2).
- **PSF:** FWHM 1.73 px = 125″ (86 stars V 5–7) — undersampled; top-
  hat photometry is the right estimator (a narrow Gaussian weight
  raises the scatter to 0.5 mag).
- **Depth vs elongation (single 40-min frame, 5σ, solar colour):**
  V 12.2 at HPLN −23°, 11.5 at −12°, 10.7 at −7°, **10.5 at −5.8°,
  9.9 at −4.5°** — the in-beam arc sits in the brightest F-corona of
  the field (28–35 DN/s/px vs 3–8 at the outer edge). The plan's "V ~
  13" is the outer-field figure; the sunward arc is ~V 10–10.5 per
  frame.

## 5. Sunward geometry in HI-1 (census result, decisive)

HI-1A images a 20° band on one side of the Sun (|HPLN| 3.9°–24.15°;
east in 2007–2014 and from 2023-08, west in the rolled 2015-11 →
2023-08 era — see the addendum). Every 0.1 AU event's in-beam arc
(b_e ≤ 0.1 AU ⇔ ε ≤ 6.0° at 0.96 AU) intersects the footprint at
**ε 4.06°–6.0°, |t − t_ca| 3.85–5.67 d — before t_ca when the camera
looks east (the direction approaches conjunction), after t_ca when it
looks west (it recedes): 44 h ≈ 66 frames per window** (gj-908 with
b = 11.4 R☉: 24 h). **274 of 295 event rows are visible** with the
measured pointing eras (the 21 others fall in the 2014–15 gap). The
grazing rungs (ε ≤ 0.7°) are never visible → `not_constrainable`.
Each source direction also transits the field for ~19 d on the arc's
side (ε 24° ↔ 6°) — a **star-fixed off-window baseline** exists for
every source, which the LASCO corona-frame construction could not
have. The helioprojective transform used for this was validated
against the test-frame header WCS to 0.0003° (0.02 px).

S2 1 AU ledger: 16 targets transit HI-1 (289 transits, ~19 d each);
the whole transit is in-beam at that rung, so no window-locked
construction exists (hypotheses D7: out of scope, ledger kept).

## 6. Pre-freeze data contact

The recon frames (2010-06-15 00:09 / 00:49 UT) lie **inside two
in-beam arcs**: gj-1276 S1 (`evt-6a6141430884`, source at pixel
(981, 510), b_e 0.079 AU) and wolf-359 S2 (`evt-d39c468c8a27`,
(957, 531), b_e 0.086 AU), both confirmatory units. Nothing was
measured at those positions (full-field star match; column-strip
noise); the hypotheses draft §11 declares the remedy: those two frame
epochs are excluded from the in-arc series of those two events, and
the recon frames are never search frames.

## 7. What a survey needs (estimate) — all drafted in `hypotheses.md`

1. Substrate: RAL L1 `14h1A` via the CGI (search), SSC L2 as fallback
   + validation. Per-frame chain: header WCS verified by a Hipparcos
   match → high-pass → 2-D colour-corrected ZP → top-hat photometry at
   ICRS-fixed patches.
2. Constructions: star-fixed differential (arc minus the same patch's
   own baseline transit, with a measured differential flat) and 8
   simultaneous parallel-track control patches (HPLT ±1..±4°), same-
   frame differential, S_stack / S_event / S_pulse, T = max over
   controls (the LASCO v1.1 shape with sky patches in place of PA
   rings — PA rings at ε 5° leave the footprint beyond ±37°).
3. Masks: synoptic-sum gate, NaN validity, planet/**Earth/Moon**
   proximity and in-FOV vetoes (Earth sits in HI-1A when the
   spacecraft–Earth separation is < 24°: 2007–08, 2022–24), Tycho-2
   VT ≤ 9 patch mask with the S2 source exemption.
4. Volume: ~80k frames ≈ 340 GB transient at full cadence (arc +
   5.7-d baseline per event; controls share frames).
5. Positive control: bright-asteroid arc passage (Horizons `@-234`);
   gj-908's null star-fixed differential in situ.

## Addendum (later the same day) — coverage intersect

`scripts/coverage_intersect.py` → `results/coverage_v1.json`
(snapshots: 7,045 SSC daily listings, 6,675 per-day headers by HTTP
Range, the RAL L1 day list; `runs/stereo-hi-crossings/coverage/`).

- **Archive inventory:** SSC L2 7,045 days (2006-12-01 → 2026-08-30),
  RAL L1 7,042 days (2006-12-13 → 2026-08-31); **234,689 frames**,
  ~12.4–13.7k/yr except 2014 (7.3k) and 2015 (1.5k). L1/L2 parity on 40
  seeded days: 1,460 L1 vs 1,437 L2 stems — 23 L1-only frames (1.6 %),
  0 L2-only; the level-1 tree is the more complete one.
- **Gaps ≥ 3 d:** 2007-03-02→07, 2007-03-14→27, 2014-07-07→10,
  **2014-08-19 → 2015-11-16 (455 d: safe mode + superior
  conjunction)**, 2018-05-24→06-05, 2023-08-07→15.
- **Pointing history (the decisive fact):** CCD centre at HPLN
  **−14° (east) 2006-12 → 2014-08-18**, **+14° (west) 2015-11-17 →
  2023-08-15** — the spacecraft flew rolled 180° after conjunction —
  and **−14° again from 2023-08-16** (after the Earth passage). HPLT
  centre drifts seasonally ±1.9° (excursions to +7° in early 2007 and
  −4.6° in 2023-08); 6,615 of 6,675 days nominal. The census had
  assumed east-looking throughout (the recon frame); with the eras
  encoded (`hi_geometry.POINTING_ERAS`) the west-era arcs fall
  **after** t_ca (the fixed direction recedes from the Sun through
  the same 4°–6° band) — the search construction is unchanged, the
  frames' own header centres decide the footprint.
- **Per-event coverage:** **272 of 295 event rows covered** (≥ 10 arc
  frames): 160 pre-t_ca + 113 post-t_ca arcs (one event straddles);
  median 65–66 arc frames (gj-908: 30–36, its arc is 24 h), 5th
  percentile 30; star-fixed baselines median ~205 frames. Per unit:
  18–20 of 21 events. Uncovered: 19 in the 2014–15 gap, 1 in the
  2007-03 gap, 2 outside the era (2007-01-01, 2026-10-23). Every day
  header sampled has N_IMAGES 30 / EXPTIME 1199.9 s.
- Volume: 614 unique arc days; arcs + baselines ≈ 2,070 frame-days ≈
  70k frames ≈ 300 GB transient at full cadence.

# PTF/iPTF recon (plan §5.8 item 3)

Goal: verify the access route for the PS1-era-parallel northern
crossings survey — epochal difference-era optical imaging over the
universal crossing windows (`crossings/universal_v1/`), channels A
(on-star) and B (antipode). The plan row said IRSA IBE
`ptf/products/` "same machinery as `irsa_ztf.py`, adapter mostly
config" and was marked _unprobed_; this recon replaces that with
endpoint facts.

Headline: **fully reachable, anonymous, and the adapter really is
mostly config** — but two plan assumptions need correction. (1) The
public archive ends **2015-01-28** (MJD 57050.6), not 2016: late-iPTF
epochal images were never publicly released, so the era is
2009-03 → 2015-01, parallel to PS1 rather than bridging to ZTF.
(2) There are **no public difference images** — level 1 is epochal
science images (+ per-exposure masks), level 2 is reference coadds;
the substrate is warp-direct-style (the PS1-crossings pattern), not
ZTF-style diff photometry.

**Science justification confirmed at recon**: the van-maanen April
deep-graze family (b ≈ 0.27–0.37 R☉ — the deepest grazes of the
programme era, lost by PS1 chip-gap masks and TESS sector gaps) has
PTF epochs within ±3 d of t_ca in 2009, 2011 and 2013, including
**9 epochs with closest 0.36 d in 2011** at the antipode.

## Level 1 facts (epochal images)

- **Era (measured, global)**: MJD 54891.1 → 57050.6 =
  **2009-03-01 → 2015-01-28**, 2,818,277 CCD exposures. iPTF
  2015–2017 is absent from the public archive.
- **Metadata columns of use**: `obsmjd obsdate obsjd exptime aexptime`
  (60 s standard), `fid/filter` (1 = g, 2 = R; H-alpha exists),
  `ccdid` (0–11; **CCD 3 dead** — absent from the level-2 tree),
  `ptffield`, `seeing airmass moonillf`, `infobits photcalflag`,
  `pfilename pchecksum`, ancillaries `anciltype1=dmask`,
  `anciltype2=sexcat` with filenames+checksums, full **PV-distortion
  WCS in metadata** (`crpix* cd* pv1_* pv2_*`) plus corners
  `ra1..dec4`, `ipac_gid ipac_pub_date`. IBE `POS/SIZE` +
  `INTERSECT=OVERLAPS` + `WHERE` + `ct=csv` behave exactly as ZTF.
- **Product path**: `pfilename` is served verbatim under
  `ibe/data/ptf/images/level1/` (e.g.
  `proc/2011/01/21/f2/c2/p13/v1/PTF_..._scie_....fits`); mask =
  `afilename1` (`..._mask_...fits`), SExtractor catalog =
  `afilename2` (`.ctlg`).
- **Image headers** (verified on a cutout): `MAGZPT` (photometric ZP;
  `PHTCALEX/PHTCALFL` flags), `LIMITMAG`, `SEEING`, `MOONILLF`,
  `GAIN 1.6`, `READNOI 5.4`, `PIXSCALE 1.01"/px`. The probed frame
  (seeing 4.9″) had LIMITMAG 18.9 — per-epoch depth varies strongly;
  the nominal R ~ 20.5–21 is a good-seeing number. Measure the
  in-window depth distribution at freeze, don't assume it.
- **Mask**: 16-bit `dmask`; probed cutout shows values {0, 2}. Bit
  definitions are **not yet pinned** — Laher et al. 2014 (PTF photometric
  pipeline) / IPAC PTF docs at freeze (open item).
- **Timing (verified)**: header `UTC-OBS = DATE-OBS =
  2011-01-21T09:00:18.527` equals `OBSMJD 55582.37521` exactly →
  **`obsmjd` = shutter-open UTC (exposure start)**;
  `t_mid = obsmjd + aexptime/2`. `OBSJD = obsmjd + 2400000.5`;
  an `HJD` keyword also present (heliocentric — do not mix).

## Level 2 facts (reference coadds)

Data tree `ibe/data/ptf/images/level2/dNNN/d<field>/f<fid>/c<ccd>/`
(field prefix dNNN = field/1000; note tree dirs use `f1/c2` while
filenames use `f01/c02`). Per chip: `refimg.fits`, `uncert.fits`,
`depcov.fits` (depth-of-coverage), `psfgrd.fits`, `daopsf.rpsf`,
`psfcat.ctlg`, `sexcat.ctlg`, `srcds9.regn`. Metadata carries
`limitmag`, `modefwhm`, input-epoch span and count. At the van-maanen
antipode: R ref from 32 inputs, g ref from 5.

## Catalog screening route

`ptf_objects` (TAP, anonymous, CSV) works with
`CONTAINS(POINT(...),CIRCLE(...))`; per-object lightcurve statistics
(mean/median/rms/percentiles, Stetson, von Neumann), `transient_flag`,
`nobs/ngoodobs`, reference mag; probed sources reach R ≈ 21.2.
Per-epoch photometry via `ptf_lightcurves`; per-frame `sexcat.ctlg`
is the deepest single-epoch list (level-1 relative photometry —
calibration caveats via `photcalflag`/`ptfphotcalcat`).

## What this means for the survey design

1. **Adapter really is mostly config** on the `irsa_ztf.py` pattern:
   same IBE search/data/cutout semantics, same WCS-from-metadata
   nominal footprint (PV → astropy TPV/SIP handled), same
   mask-product exact footprint. Differences: column names, `pfilename`
   used verbatim (no path construction), published MD5 checksums,
   12-CCD (not quadrant) geometry, no `imgtype`/gid filtering needed.
2. **Substrate = warp-direct** (PS1-crossings pattern): forced
   photometry on epochal science images with the level-2 reference as
   the static-sky subtraction/veto, not archive difference images.
   The PS1-crossings statistic machinery is the transfer template.
3. **The headline cell is the van-maanen April deep-graze family** —
   the same family ATLAS hypotheses v1.0 targets (b = 0.24 R☉,
   ATLAS era 2015+) and SOHO deepens to 0.13 R☉: PTF adds the
   **2009/2011/2013 pre-ATLAS instances** that PS1 masks destroyed.
   Cross-era recurrence with ATLAS (once its server run happens) is
   the natural joint statement.
4. Teegarden is structurally out (zero coverage); wolf-359 and
   gj-1276 antipodes have epochs but none in-window at ±3 d —
   expect the searched-unit list to be small (van-maanen +
   ross-128 dominated). Frame the survey accordingly: few units,
   deep-graze rung, not broad coverage.
5. Runs on the dev machine fine — cutout-sized pulls, no queue
   drain, no auth. No server-migration dependency (unlike ATLAS).
